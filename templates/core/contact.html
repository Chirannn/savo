Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is Savo Mart grocery delivery?

We deliver within 2 hours for all standard express orders placed between 6:00 AM and 10:00 PM. You can also pick a specific preferred delivery slot at checkout.

What payment options do you support?

We support all major payment options including instant UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm QR), Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard), and Cash on Delivery (COD).

What if a grocery item is damaged or not fresh?

Savo Mart offers an instant no-questions-asked doorstep refund or replacement. Simply notify our delivery executive or submit a request under your Order History.
